The Babel Fish

A small yellow pixel-art fish lives in the bottom-right corner of the screen, out of the aquarium that serves as the wallpaper. Like his namesake in the Hitchhiker’s Guide, he understands every language spoken to him — and his body tells, in 72 × 56 pixels, everything Jarvis is doing or feeling.
